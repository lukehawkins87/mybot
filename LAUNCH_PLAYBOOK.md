# Masterclass Launch Playbook

> Offer: **"Buy a business"** masterclass (3-day live), run in Australia & USA.
> Goal of this doc: turn the raw notes into clear, delegatable workstreams.
> Backend offer value: **$8,000**. Setters earn **5% commission on backend only** (= **$400 per sale**).

---

## How to use this doc
Each section below is a **workstream** with:
- **Owner** — who you delegate it to
- **What it is** — plain-English purpose
- **Do this** — the concrete steps
- **Open decisions** — things you still need to decide (marked ⚠️)

The 8 workstreams:
1. Setter Calling Playbook
2. Setter Pay & Headcount Math
3. WhatsApp Community ("Concierge")
4. The Workbook (+ password rollout)
5. VIP Upsell
6. Live Event / Speaker Playbook
7. Data & AI Analysis (ICPs, avatars, who NOT to target)
8. Next Launch Build (ads, pages, sequences)

## 1. Setter Calling Playbook
**Owner:** Head Setter / Sales Lead (3 setters total)

**What it is:** The phone team that calls leads to (a) get non-buyers to buy a ticket, (b) upsell non-VIP buyers into VIP, and (c) confirm attendance. Just talking to a setter lifted non-buyer → ticket conversion by **+4%**.

**Do this — for non-VIP buyers (the core 4-step process):**
1. **Triage / qualify verbally** on the call.
2. **Book them in now** — add a calendar event live on the call, and **send the SMS during the call** so they add the event themselves.
3. **Send content** — about the founder ("around me") and about what they specifically talked about in triage. If qualified, a future call gets booked.
4. **VIP upsell** — "I noticed you didn't get VIP — you mentioned X, Y, Z; VIP will help with exactly that."

**Do this — calling cadence:**
- After **Day 1**: call **attendees AND non-attendees**. Tell attendees to show up for Day 2.
- **Day before the event**: call **every purchaser to confirm**. Target was ~**300 calls per setter**; some cascaded. (Last run: attendance still dropped ~10 people — see if confirmation calls can close that gap.)
- **Message everyone after hours** (don't only work 9–5).
- **Prioritise high-ICP leads first** (see Workstream 7).
- **Prioritise by workbook completion** — call those who filled out the **most** workbooks → least.

**Second-call structure (post Day 1):**
1. "How did you find the information?"
2. "How did you get on with the first day?"
3. (Some will ask for replays — handle per policy below.)

**Policies:**
- If people ask **for the workbook** outside the process: "we're not doing that" — it's earned through the live, not handed out.
- **Quality control:** record all calls and **coach setters** off the recordings.

**Onboarding when they buy:** walk them through the process → **get them into the WhatsApp group**.

**⚠️ Open decisions:**
- Exact qualification criteria for "qualified" in step 1.
- Replay policy — who gets them, when.

## 2. Setter Pay & Headcount Math
**Owner:** You / Finance

**What it is:** Decide how the 3 full-time setters are paid and model the numbers.

**Do this:**
- Current: **5% of $8k backend = $400/sale**, backend only.
- **Model commission-only** vs base+commission: how many sales each setter needs/month to earn a livable wage.
- Build a simple sheet: `setters × calls/day × conversion × $400` → projected earnings vs. cost.

**⚠️ Open decisions:**
- Commission-only or hybrid?
- Do they earn on front-end ticket sales too, or strictly backend?

## 3. WhatsApp Community ("Concierge")
**Owner:** Community Manager

**What it is:** A group where buyers are framed as **"concierge members"** and warmed up before/through the event.

**Do this:**
- **1 pinned video** at the top — a **selfie-style** video.
- Post **2–3 videos per day** in the group for the **3 days** before the event.
- Keep active messaging to the **last 3 days** only (don't over-message earlier).
- Frame everyone as **concierge members** (premium feel).
- On the **day of**, post the "final task" message (see Workbook section): event is in your calendar + here's your workbook + here's your access code.

## 4. The Workbook (+ Password Rollout)
**Owner:** Speaker + Slide/Deck Owner

**What it is:** A workbook (really a **survey** — see questions below) given to all attendees. Completing it is the qualifier the setters use, and it feeds VIP/data.

**Do this — access & password rollout (this got messy last time, fix it):**
- Workbook lives on **slide 6** with a **QR code + link**.
- Speaker must **say WHY the workbook matters** (this was missing).
- **Reveal the password ~3/4 of the way through Day 1**, on screen for **~1 minute only**, **live-only** (not in replay).
- Put a note in the slide corner: **"please remove password from slides."**
- **Remove the password segment from the replay.**
- Fix from last time: at end of Session 1 everyone was confused about the password — pre-empt it.

**Completion incentive:**
- "If you fill out **all 3 workbooks**, you get access to the **AI workbook** we built."

**Survey / workbook questions:**
- What's pulling you towards building a business?
- Before today, how confident were you that you could buy a business? After today, how confident?
- "5 questions you need to buy a business" — which sector are you drawn to exploring?
- How much time do you need to put in, based on what you learned today?
- (Dee) "Always be the buyer — picture yourself as a buyer." What's the 1 question you'd ask?
- Be honest: what is most likely to stop you from doing this?
- What would owning a business do for you and your family? Picture it.

**⚠️ Open decision:** Do you want to *force* both the workbook **and** survey completion, or keep them combined as one?

## 5. VIP Upsell
**Owner:** Speaker + Setters

**What it is:** The premium tier setters upsell and the speaker promotes live.

**VIP includes:**
- A **1-on-1 day with the founder** (as a group, to ask questions).
- Entry into a **giveaway for a private 1-on-1 with the founder**.
- **Extra training** bolted onto the program.
- **Broken-down case studies** ("glorified case studies that break it down").
- A **formula calculator**.

**Do this — promotion rhythm:**
- **Start of each day:** "If you want VIP, if you have questions, come to VIP."
- Mention VIP **frequently on Day 2 and Day 3**.
- Add a VIP mention at the **beginning of Day 3** (currently underused).

## 6. Live Event / Speaker Playbook
**Owner:** Speaker / Host

**What it is:** Scripts and framing to maximise engagement and attendance across the 3 days.

**Cameras-on framing (scripted):**
- "We're forcing everyone to put cameras on."
- "If your camera's off, we know you want to do 2–3 things at once."
- "What does it take to be successful? Focus, concentration."
- "Our job is to keep you accountable so you become successful."
- "You've done training before, left it, felt flat — was that the training or you? Put the camera on."
- "There's going to be back-and-forth. If I need to speak to one of you, I need to see your face."
- **Privately message** people to turn their camera on.

**Day 1 "teenager" framing:**
- "What does someone successful do? Do they show up?"
- "What do teenagers do when they disagree / don't get their way?"
- "By the end of today, none of us act like teenagers."
- "I know the people in this room are intelligent, smart people."

**Guest speakers:**
- Bring a **guest success story / testimonial each day** (Day 1 had a guest as testimonial — repeat across all days).

**Timing:**
- Sessions scheduled 90 min ran ~2 hrs — frame it: "this may go over because we want to give you value."

**Attendance benchmark (watch the drop-off):**
- Last run: **960 → 940** after Day 1 (~20 lost). Setter confirmation calls (Workstream 1) exist to shrink this.

## 7. Data & AI Analysis (ICPs, Avatars, Targeting)
**Owner:** You / Data + AI

**What it is:** Use past launch data to define exactly who to target (and who NOT to), and the language that converts.

**Do this — inputs to gather:**
- All **buyers** from the **last 2 AUS launches** and **last 2 USA launches**.
- **All call transcripts** of buyers who took calls from those launches.

**Do this — AI outputs to produce:**
1. **Client avatar** per market: circumstances, problems, desires, and other key data points.
2. **Language map**: the words/phrases buyers used → use in copy.
3. **Top 3 ICPs for AUS** and **Top 3 ICPs for USA** (they differ — e.g., coaches converted well in USA but not AUS).
4. **Who NOT to target.**
5. **Positioning angle:** how to attract these people **without calling it a certification**.
6. **Equal-volume prompt:** "If lead volume were equal across all these categories, which ICPs win?" — so ICP ranking isn't skewed by current traffic mix.

**Feeds into:** 3 separate **WhatsApp groups**, one per ICP, so messaging can call out each ICP specifically.

## 8. Next Launch Build (Ads, Pages, Sequences)
**Owner:** Marketing

**What it is:** The next masterclass runs **~2–3 weeks after this one** — build the funnel for it now, segmented by ICP.

**Do this — for each of the 3 ICPs × 2 markets (AUS + USA):**
- **Film ads** (3 ICPs × AUS masterclass, 3 ICPs × US masterclass).
- **Landing pages** per ICP.
- **Email sequences** per ICP.
- **Nurture / "what" sequences** per ICP.
- **Tech setup** to support the above.

**Depends on:** Workstream 7 (ICP definitions and language) must finish first.
