The Netlify auto-deploy problem, solved

This page was built and published to Netlify by Claude in a single step. The fact that you're reading it at a live URL is the proof.

What was going wrong

When Claude built a website and pushed it to GitHub, the commits were authored as Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>. Netlify builds your sites from GitHub, and it checks who authored the commit behind each build.

Because "Claude" isn't a member of your Netlify team, Netlify held every deploy in a Pending approval state — a security and billing safeguard for deploys triggered by non-team members — and waited for you to approve each one by hand.

The one-time fix

  1. Open Team settings → Access & security → Visitor access
  2. Find Auto-approve deploys from non-team members
  3. Click Edit settings, choose Auto-approve, save

After that, GitHub-triggered deploys build and publish with no approval step. On the Free plan there's no billing downside.

The instant path (used for this page)

Claude can also deploy directly through your connected Netlify account instead of going through GitHub. Direct deploys are authenticated as you, so they never hit the approval queue — which is exactly how this page went live automatically.
