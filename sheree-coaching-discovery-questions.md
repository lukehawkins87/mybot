# Sheree — Business Coaching Discovery Questions

**Client:** Sheree Agnew
**Businesses:** NDIS mental health & disability support company (~30 staff, hiring 5 more) + equine assisted therapy + emerging side coaching business
**Headline goal:** $1,000,000 profit / year (currently ~$100k profit over 6 months — roughly a 5x lift)
**Deeper goal:** Delegate her way out of the day-to-day so she gets **time freedom** while the business becomes **highly profitable** — then step sideways into coaching and "have fun and live life."

> **How to use this:** Send Sheree these questions (Whispr voice-to-text is fine — she can just talk her answers out). She doesn't need to write essays. The aim is to get a complete, honest picture of the numbers, the team, the growth levers, and how her days are actually spent, so the follow-up strategy session goes straight to building the plan.

> **Important context to hold while coaching:** Sheree is *deliberately* keeping the NDIS profit low right now on her accountant's advice — until her ex relocates to America (~2 months) and her daughter finishes study — to manage a child-support situation. So the "$1M profit" target is really about **building the profit-generating capacity** now (systems, margins, structure) and choosing *when* to let that profit show. Frame the growth work around that, not against it.

## Section 1 — Current Numbers (The Baseline)

We can't build a plan to $1M without a clean picture of what's happening today. Answer with your best current figures — estimates are fine, we'll tighten them later.

**Revenue**
1. What is your NDIS business bringing in per month right now, on average (cash in / invoiced)?
2. How has that trended over the last 12 months — growing, flat, or dropped off during the head-injury period? Roughly what did each of those months look like?
3. What's your split of revenue by service type (e.g. SIL, community access, in-home support, independent living skills, equine therapy, etc.)? Which line is biggest?
4. How much of your revenue is "locked in" recurring (ongoing participant plans) vs one-off or ad-hoc?

**Costs & margin**
5. What are your total monthly expenses, broken into: wages/labour, rent/property, vehicles, admin/software, and everything else?
6. What's your labour cost as a % of revenue right now? (Shift Care should give you this.)
7. You mentioned casual rates are chewing your margins — how many staff are casual vs permanent part-time/full-time, and what would moving some to permanent do to your cost line?
8. What is your true net profit margin on the NDIS business over the last 12 months (before the deliberate reinvestment/toys/structuring)?
9. What does Shift Care tell you about where you're **over-charging or under-charging**? Which services are most and least profitable per hour?

**Cash & obligations**
10. What's your current cash position and how many weeks of runway does it represent?
11. What debts are outstanding (the ~$200k — what's it made of, what are the repayments, what interest)?
12. What are your fixed weekly/monthly commitments regardless of revenue (rents ~$2k/wk, car ~$600/wk, horse feed ~$4k/mo, etc.)?

**Tracking**
13. Before the injury you checked numbers 2–3x a week. What's the minimum dashboard you want to see weekly to feel in control again — and who could prepare it for you so you just *review* it?
14. Which numbers, if they moved, would you *feel* the fastest in profit? (This tells us where to focus first.)

## Section 2 — Team Structure

The whole plan hinges on the team carrying the business without you in every seat.

15. Draw me your current org chart: who are your ~30 people, what roles, and who reports to whom?
16. Is there anyone today operating as a **2IC / operations manager** — someone who could run the day-to-day if you disappeared for a month? If not, who's the closest?
17. Which roles are currently sitting on *your* plate that shouldn't be (payroll, accounting, rostering, invoicing, participant intake, HR)?
18. What does your hiring plan for the next 5 people look like — what roles, and why those?
19. Where are the single points of failure — jobs only *you* know how to do, or only one other person knows how to do?
20. How documented are your processes? Do you have SOPs / a manual, or is it mostly in people's heads?
21. What's your current staff turnover like, and what does it cost you to replace someone?
22. Who on the team is A-player material you'd want to promote and lean on more — and who's a drag you're tolerating?
23. What's your management rhythm — team meetings, 1:1s, KPIs? Or is it reactive/ad-hoc right now?

## Section 3 — High / Medium / Low-Value Task Audit (Delegation Engine)

This is the core of the time-freedom plan. Be brutally honest — the goal is to get the low-value work *off you*.

24. Walk me through a typical work day and a typical work week, hour by hour. What do you actually spend time on?
25. **High-value tasks** — What are the handful of things only *you* can do that most move revenue, profit, or strategy? (e.g. big-picture direction, key relationships, high-level decisions, selling/growth.)
26. **Medium-value tasks** — What do you do that matters but that a trained manager or senior staffer *could* do with the right systems? (e.g. rostering decisions, hiring interviews, participant escalations.)
27. **Low-value tasks** — What are you still doing that is admin, repetitive, or below your pay grade and should be delegated, automated, or deleted entirely? (e.g. payroll, data entry, chasing invoices, answering routine emails.)
28. Of everything on that list — what do you actually *enjoy* and get energy from, vs what drains you? (We delegate the drains first.)
29. If you could only work **10 hours a week** on the NDIS business, which tasks would you keep and which would have to go to someone else?
30. What's stopping you from handing each low-value task over *today* — is it trust, cost, no one trained, or a belief that "it won't get done right"?
31. You said you had a belief that if you stepped away the business would crumble. On a scale of 1–10, how true does that feel now — and what would need to be true for it to be a 1?
32. What would you *do with the freed-up time* — more coaching, family, health, travel, strategy? (Anchoring this makes the delegation stick.)

## Section 4 — Lead Generation & Marketing

How do new participants / clients find you today, and how do we turn that into a predictable machine?

33. Where do your NDIS participants currently come from? (Support coordinators, word of mouth, referrals, online, plan managers, allied health partners?)
34. Do you actively market, or is growth mostly referral/reputation right now?
35. What's your #1 source of new participants — and if it dried up tomorrow, what would you do?
36. Who are your best referral partners (support coordinators, LACs, hospitals, allied health), and how strong are those relationships?
37. What's your online presence — website, Google Business, social, reviews? How much does it bring in?
38. Do you track how many enquiries you get per month and where each came from? (If not, this is a quick win.)
39. What's your reputation/differentiator in the market — why do participants and coordinators choose *you* over another provider?
40. Geographically, where do you operate now, and where do you want to expand (you mentioned SIL houses in NSW)?
41. If we wanted to *double* qualified enquiries in 90 days, what's the most obvious lever you're not pulling?
42. What's your budget and appetite for marketing spend vs relationship/referral-based growth?

## Section 5 — Sales & Conversion

Getting the enquiry is half the game — converting it is the other half.

43. When an enquiry comes in, what happens step by step until they become a paying participant? Who handles it?
44. Roughly what % of enquiries convert into active participants? How long does it take?
45. Where do people drop off or go cold in that process?
46. Who does the "selling"/intake conversations — you, or someone on the team? How consistent is it?
47. Is there a documented intake/onboarding process, or does it vary depending on who handles it?
48. What's your average revenue per participant, and their average lifetime (how long they stay with you)?
49. What causes participants to leave, and what's your retention/win-back approach?
50. Are there services you *could* offer existing participants that you don't (upsell/cross-sell within their plans)?
51. What would need to change for conversion to be handled entirely without you touching it?

## Section 6 — Scaling the NDIS Business Toward $1M Profit

52. What does the path from ~$150k/mo revenue to $1M profit/year actually look like in your head — more participants, higher-margin services, new locations, SIL houses?
53. **SIL houses** — what's the model, the setup cost, the timeline, and the profit per house? How many houses would it take to move the needle?
54. What are the regulatory / registration / compliance hurdles to expanding into NSW, and who handles that?
55. Which of your services has the **highest profit margin per hour** — and could you deliberately grow *that* one faster?
56. What's the constraint that's actually capping growth right now — staff, systems, your time, cash, or demand?
57. You mentioned wanting to "invest business money into something bigger" but weren't sure what. What are the options on the table, and what's the return/risk of each? (Let's get specific rather than "something bigger.")
58. If you did *nothing new* but just tightened margins (casual→permanent, right-charging, cutting the equine money leak), how much extra profit is sitting there already?

## Section 7 — The Equine Business (Stop the Bleeding First)

Right now this looks like a money leak, not a business. Let's decide: fix it, shrink it, or exit it.

59. What does the equine operation cost you all-in per month (16 personal horses ~$1,500/wk, the 4 stuck horses ~$325/wk, feed ~$4k/mo, property, your time)?
60. What does it *earn* per month ($600–700/wk of therapy income, $150 1:1, $80–150/person groups of 4–8)? Is it net positive or negative?
61. The 4 horses you legally can't sell yet — when *does* that restriction lift, and what's the plan the day it does?
62. Could any of the horses' costs be charged through NDIS-funded equine therapy legitimately, so they earn their keep?
63. The plan to lease/agist paddocks at ~$100/paddock — what's blocking it now, and what would it take to turn that into real income?
64. Honestly: does the equine business serve the $1M goal and the time-freedom goal, or is it a passion/lifestyle piece that needs to be ring-fenced so it stops draining the profitable business?
65. If you ran the numbers and equine was costing you $X/month net — at what number would you downsize the herd or exit?

## Section 8 — The Side Coaching Business (Getting Clarity)

You've already landed your first client at $7k. This could be the vehicle for time freedom *and* the "fun" you want. Let's get clear before we scale it.

66. When you picture the coaching business in 2 years, what does it actually look like — 1:1, groups, retreats, online program, women's frameworks, something else?
67. You dabble between the **spiritual world** and the **clinical/CEO world**. If you had to pick *one* lane to lead with (and use the other as depth/support), which lights you up more and which does the market pay for?
68. Who is your ideal coaching client — the exact person you're best placed to help and most want to work with? (e.g. high-achieving women, women in business, trauma/self-worth, health?)
69. What transformation do you deliver that people would happily pay $7k+ for? What's the "before → after"?
70. You've got a stack of modalities (mental health counselling, arts therapy, equine psychotherapy, Gestalt, sound healing, meditation, plus the recent breakthrough work). Which of those actually get *results* you believe in — and which are "dust collectors" you can let go of?
71. You already run women's groups and frameworks — what's working there, and could that be the backbone of a scalable group program?
72. What would you charge, in what format (1:1 / group / program / mastermind), and how many clients would give you the income *and* the lifestyle you want?
73. How do you want coaching clients to find you — your network/reputation, referrals, social, speaking? (You said you want to network with capable people so you can go solve their problems — is that the lead engine?)
74. How much time per week do you *want* coaching to take, given it's meant to be the "fun, live life" part — not another business that owns you?
75. What's your unfair advantage as a coach that no one else has? (Your story, your directness/"I'll tell you how it is", your business track record, your depth of modalities?)

## Section 9 — Vision, Structure & Time Freedom (Tying It Together)

76. 12 months from now, if this all worked, describe your ideal week: where are you, how many hours are you working, on what, and who's running the rest?
77. Of your three ventures (NDIS, equine, coaching) — which is the **profit engine**, which is the **lifestyle/passion piece**, and which is the **freedom vehicle**? Do you agree with that framing?
78. What's your personal income vs business profit target — how much do you actually need/want landing in *your* pocket each month, and by when?
79. What's the single biggest thing standing between you and stepping back from the NDIS day-to-day?
80. Post head-injury, what are your non-negotiables now around health, energy, and hours — the boundaries the plan has to protect?
81. If we could only fix **three things** in the next 90 days to move you toward $1M profit *and* time freedom, what would you want them to be?
82. What does "done" look like — how will you *know* you've built the highly profitable business with the time freedom you're after?
