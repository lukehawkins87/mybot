# SOP: Ensuring Fathom Records Every Zoom Sales Call

**Purpose:** Guarantee that Fathom starts recording on every sales call so no meeting goes unrecorded.

---

## Before the Call — Setup Checklist

1. **Confirm Fathom is installed and connected**
   - Open [fathom.video](https://fathom.video) and verify you are logged in.
   - Confirm your Google Calendar is connected to Fathom (Settings > Integrations).
   - Confirm your Zoom account is connected to Fathom (Settings > Integrations).

2. **Verify the meeting appears in Fathom's upcoming calls list**
   - In the Fathom dashboard, check that the upcoming meeting shows under "Upcoming Calls."
   - If it does not appear, manually add the Zoom link in Fathom before the call.

3. **Keep Google Calendar open in your browser before the call starts**
   - Do not rely on the Zoom app's "upcoming meetings" list to launch calls.

---

## Joining the Call — The Reliable Method

> **Always join Zoom from the Google Calendar invite link — not from the Zoom app directly.**

This is the single most important step. Launching Zoom from directly inside the Zoom app can cause Fathom to miss the meeting and fail to join. Launching from the Google Calendar link consistently triggers Fathom to join.

**Step-by-step:**

1. Open **Google Calendar** in your browser.
2. Click on the calendar event for the sales call.
3. Inside the event, click the **Zoom link** (e.g., `zoom.us/j/...`).
4. Zoom will launch and you will be placed in the meeting.
5. Within 30–60 seconds, **Fathom will automatically join as a participant** and begin recording.

**Do not:**
- Click "Join" from the Zoom desktop app's home screen or upcoming meetings list.
- Open Zoom first and then search for the meeting ID manually.

---

## During the Call — Confirm Fathom Is Recording

1. Look for **Fathom in the participant list** — it will appear as "Fathom Notetaker" or similar.
2. Check for a **recording indicator** (red dot) in the Zoom toolbar.
3. If Fathom has not joined within 60 seconds, go to the **Troubleshooting** section below immediately — do not wait until the call ends.

---

## Troubleshooting — If Fathom Does Not Join

Work through these steps in order:

### Step 1 — Manually invite Fathom mid-call
1. Open [fathom.video](https://fathom.video) in your browser.
2. Click **"Record a Meeting"** or **"Join Current Call."**
3. Paste the Zoom meeting link and submit — Fathom will join within 30 seconds.

### Step 2 — Check your Fathom auto-record settings
1. Go to Fathom Settings > Recording.
2. Confirm **"Auto-record all meetings"** (or equivalent) is enabled.
3. Confirm the calendar Fathom is monitoring matches the calendar the event lives on.

### Step 3 — Check Fathom's calendar sync
1. In Fathom, go to Settings > Integrations > Google Calendar.
2. Disconnect and reconnect the calendar.
3. Verify the meeting now appears in Fathom's upcoming calls list.

### Step 4 — Restart the Fathom desktop app (if installed)
1. Quit the Fathom app completely.
2. Relaunch it.
3. Re-join the Zoom call using the Google Calendar link method above.

### Step 5 — Re-join the Zoom meeting
1. If none of the above work, leave and rejoin the Zoom meeting using the Google Calendar link.
2. Fathom should detect the new session and join automatically.

### Step 6 — Record locally as a backup
If Fathom still fails to join and the call is in progress:
1. Use Zoom's built-in recording: press **Alt+R** (Windows) or **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac).
2. Notify your manager immediately after the call so the recording can be processed manually.

---

## After the Call

1. Confirm the recording appears in your Fathom dashboard within a few minutes of the call ending.
2. If it does not appear within 15 minutes, contact Fathom support and your manager with the meeting date/time and attendee names.
