# SOP: Finding Zoom Recordings for All Users

**Purpose:** This guide shows every team member how to locate Zoom cloud recordings for **anyone in our Zoom account** — not just their own — and how to look up who our team members are so you know which host to search for.

**Who this is for:** All team members.

**What you need:** A login to our Zoom account via the web portal ([zoom.us](https://zoom.us)) with access to Account Management. If you can't see the menus described below, ask an account admin to adjust your role permissions.

---

## Part 1: Open the Recording and Transcript Management page

This is the central page where recordings from **all users** in our account appear (unlike your personal "Recordings" page, which only shows your own).

1. Go to [zoom.us](https://zoom.us) in your browser and **sign in**.
2. In the **left-hand sidebar**, scroll down and click **Account Management** to expand it.
3. In the expanded list, click **Recording and Transcript Management** (it will highlight in blue when selected).

> 📍 The sidebar path is: **Account Management → Recording and Transcript Management**
>
> Don't confuse this with other similarly named items in the sidebar (e.g. Clip Management, Video Management, Summary Management). You want **Recording and Transcript Management** specifically.

4. Make sure you're on the **Recordings** tab at the top of the page (the other tabs are My Notes recordings, Transcripts, and Trash).

You'll now see a table of every cloud recording in the account, with columns for:

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Thumbnail** | Preview image and recording length (e.g. 00:05:30) |
| **Name** | The meeting title (e.g. "Shannon Campbell: OM: Life Coach Interview") |
| **Meeting ID** | The numeric ID of the meeting |
| **Host** | The email address of the team member who hosted the meeting |
| **Start time** | Date and time the meeting began |
| **Participants** | Who attended |
| **File size** | Number of files and total size |

> ⏳ If a recording shows **"Processing..."** on its thumbnail, it just finished and isn't ready to view yet. Check back a little later.

## Part 2: Search for a specific recording

At the top of the recordings table there are three search tools. Use whichever fits what you know about the recording you're looking for:

### Option A — Search by name or Meeting ID
Use the search box labelled **"Search by name or meeting ID"** (the middle box at the top).

- Type part of the **meeting name** — e.g. typing `Life Coach` would find "Monica Villarreal: SN: Life Coach Information Call" and "Shannon Campbell: OM: Life Coach Interview".
- Or paste the **Meeting ID** (e.g. `310 184 3597`) if you have it from a calendar invite or booking.

This is the fastest option when you know the client's name or the meeting title.

### Option B — Search inside the transcript
Use the box labelled **"Search text in audio transcript"** (to the right of the name search).

- Type a word or phrase that was **spoken during the meeting** (e.g. a client's name, a product, a topic).
- Zoom will return recordings whose audio transcript contains that text.

Use this when you don't remember the meeting name but remember what was discussed.

### Option C — Filter by group
Use the **"Select a group"** dropdown (far left) to narrow the list to recordings from a specific user group, if we have groups set up.

### Option D — Advanced search
Click **"Advanced search"** (the blue link next to the search boxes) to filter by things like:

- **Date range** — narrow to when the meeting happened
- **Host** — show only recordings from a specific team member (paste their email address from Part 3 below)

> 💡 **To find all recordings by one team member:** use Advanced search and filter by their email as Host, or simply scan the **Host** column in the table (e.g. `brad@lukehawkins.com`, `dan@lukehawkins.com`).

### Exporting a list
If you need a spreadsheet of recordings (e.g. for reporting), click the blue **Export** button at the top right of the table.

---

## Part 3: Look up who our team members are (Users section)

If you're not sure of a team member's email address (which is what appears in the **Host** column), look them up in the Users section:

1. In the **left-hand sidebar**, find **User Management** and click it to expand.
2. Click **Users**.
3. You'll see a list of everyone in our Zoom account, including each person's **name**, **email address**, role, and license type.
4. Copy the email address of the person whose recordings you want, then go back to **Account Management → Recording and Transcript Management** and search or filter by that email as the Host (Part 2, Option D).

## Quick reference

| I want to... | Do this |
|---|---|
| See all recordings in the account | Account Management → Recording and Transcript Management → Recordings tab |
| Find a recording by client/meeting name | Type the name into "Search by name or meeting ID" |
| Find a recording by what was said | Type a phrase into "Search text in audio transcript" |
| Find all recordings by one team member | Advanced search → filter by Host email (or scan the Host column) |
| Find a team member's email address | User Management → Users |
| Recover a deleted recording | Recording and Transcript Management → **Trash** tab |

## Troubleshooting

- **"I only see my own recordings."** You're probably on your personal Recordings page. Make sure you navigated via **Account Management → Recording and Transcript Management**. If you don't have that menu item, you need an admin to grant your role access to recording management.
- **"The recording isn't showing up."** It may still be processing (see the "Processing..." thumbnail), or the host may have recorded locally to their computer instead of to the cloud. Only **cloud** recordings appear here.
- **"Transcript search finds nothing."** Transcripts are only generated when audio transcription is enabled for the meeting, and they take a while to process after the meeting ends.
