The Story

A life rebuilt from the floor of a van.

Rock bottom · 2014

At 27, Luke was back in his mother's spare room, teaching casual PE for $300 a week and carrying $34,000 in debt. A brutal breakup had left him in the darkest place he'd ever been. And yet — even at his lowest — people instinctively came to him for guidance.

The bet

He made a decision most people never would. He emptied his bank account down to his last $300, bought a $1,000 van, and parked it at Bondi Beach — living out of it, eating raw vegetables, showering at the beach, all to afford a coaching course he couldn't otherwise pay for.

"This might be where I start… but it won't be where I finish."

One night in that van, it landed: his circumstances were never the problem. His mindset was. His very first client quit smoking in a single session and never went back — and the fuse was lit.

The climb

Within six months he'd quit his job. Within three years he'd earned his first million. Today he runs a $5-million-a-year business, has personally coached more than 3,500 people, and has trained over 4,400 coaches around the world — building the exact life he once imagined from the driver's seat of a $1,000 van.

Professional Background

Where he comes from.

Luke's professional life began in the classroom — a university-educated high-school PE teacher in Sydney, working casual shifts for as little as $300 a week. In 2014, a personal collapse became the catalyst for a complete reinvention: he retrained as a coach and never looked back.

Today he's the founder and CEO of his own life-coaching school — recognised as Australia's #1 — and the creator of Neuro Transformation Therapy™, a certification now trusted by coaches across the world. He works as a Master Coach Instructor and has been featured in Forbes Australia.

Behind the method is more than $1.2 million invested in his own training over twelve years — a self-directed education in the science of how people change.
