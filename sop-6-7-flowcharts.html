SOP 6 — Full Message Script Option 1 · Full Conversational Sequence
Category 1 — Showed up · Did NOT book
Stage 1 · Introduce yourself
Hey {{contact.first_name}} how's it going, it's Fernando from Luke Hawkins Life Coaching Certification School, how are you? I see you attended the Masterclass training on How to Quit your job in 90 days and make $30k/month as a Coach using AI and Neuro Transformation Therapy. Great to have you part of it, how did you find it all?
If negative response
Just thank them for their feedback and say we wish them the best, and we'll have other offers down the track of other things they might like.
If positive response
"Oh great, that's good, glad to hear!"
Stage 2 · Get their goals
Can I ask what you were looking to get out of it, was it mainly for… • Personal transformation? or • Learning coaching skills? or • Starting a coaching business / Helping others? or • A career change into coaching? Or a combination of all of the above?
They respond → "That's good. We have a lot of people joining us who are in a similar boat. We actually have over 630+ six-figure coaches now, 40+ seven-figure coaches who all started in a similar place."
Can I ask what work you are doing at the moment?
Link their background / skills to how they could use it in coaching.
That sounds good. Actually, we have a lot of students who start in that background and transition into coaching. Did you have a program already built to sell that you've already sold before?
I see, so you don't have a program yet. Did you have a bit of a social media following already? Or just starting out? And did you have a clear idea of what your niche was already or have any ideas around that? And how soon would you say you are looking to get out of your career or transition into full-time coaching? And how did you feel about getting some extra knowledge on how to get clients online?
Stage 3 · Qualify for an interview
Okay great, well you're actually in a really powerful place to launch, and I think we could definitely help further to create more of a personalised plan based on where you're at. What did you think of the idea of getting further help from us in terms of the Neuro Transformation Therapy training and/or the Seven Figure Academy social media business training?
If positive response
Answer any questions they may have. Then get them booked in for an interview. Mention testimonials relevant to their situation — people who broke free of their 9–5 and became a full-time coach.
If negative response
Just thank them for their feedback and say we wish them the best, and we'll have other offers down the track of other things they might like.
Financial Qualify
So we have different levels of support that we offer. The more support that we offer, the faster people achieve their goals, and normally the bigger the size of the goals that they achieve. Can I ask, if we were to help you to get to $30k per month within the next 90 days or less, were you in the place where you would access:
Under $1,000 → NOT QUALIFIED
$1,000 – $3,000
$3,000 – $6,000
$6,000+ → HIGHLY QUALIFIED
If not qualified (under $1k)
Okay I understand — we're probably going to run this Masterclass again at some point, so just keep an eye on your emails and texts. In the meantime, check out Luke's Instagram and YouTube channel at Luke Hawkins for more free content. Thanks a lot!
If qualified ($1k+)
Okay no worries. You actually qualify for one of the interviews. I've been allowed to give away three in the next 24 hours before they expire. Will you free some time in the next day or two — between [suggest times]?
Stage 4 · Book them in
Great, we have you booked in at [time]. You should see it in your Google Calendar — can you see the invite and Zoom link? I'm excited for you and seeing what you bring to life. I look forward to hearing how it all goes. Let me know if you have any questions along the way. Have a great call!
EOC Process — End of Conversation
  1. Update the stage in the pipeline to the correct stage
  2. Save the client notes taken
  3. Announce in the #live-events Slack channel: who you booked + which closer it went to
