Key Objection — "Is this recognised / accredited?"

Life coaching is an unregulated industry (unlike medicine, law or financial brokerage). The coaches who get the most clients are those who get the best results — which is exactly what we train for. Luke will go deeper into this in the training.

Objection — "Call me back later, I'm busy"

Are they genuinely interested in $30k/month as a coach?
NO
No problem — keep an eye on emails. END.
YES
Ask: Do they have a program ($2k–$5k) to sell? → Niche? → Why do they want this? → Current job?
Then loop back to financial qualify and book interview.
