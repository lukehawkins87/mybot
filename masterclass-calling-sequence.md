# 10-Day Masterclass Launch Calling Sequence

**Key rules:**
- Double dial = 2 calls back-to-back within a few minutes of each other
- "Newest leads" = any leads that came in since the last calling session
- Leave a message only after the 2nd unanswered call in a session (never after the 1st)
- Message scripts: **Voicemail Script** (SOP 3, Day 1 only) | **Text/WhatsApp Script 1** (SOP 3, Days 2–10)
- All non-answerers roll forward and continue being double-dialled every day until they pick up

## Notes on Message Script Usage

|---|---|

> **Note:** With only 2 scripts across 10 days, some leads will receive Text/WhatsApp Script 1 multiple times. See the options below for how to handle this.

# OPTIONS

---

## Option 1 — Add a Third Script for Later-Stage Follow-Up *(Minimal Change)*

**The issue:** Leads who haven't picked up by Day 5+ will start receiving the same Text/WhatsApp Script 1 for the 3rd or 4th time. This reduces impact.

**The fix:** Write one additional "urgency" version of the text script specifically for Days 7–10 ("The Masterclass is in X days — last chance to speak before we begin"). This script replaces Script 1 only for leads who have already received it twice or more.

Everything else in the 10-day sequence stays exactly the same.

---

## Option 2 — Add a Midday Session from Day 5 Instead of Day 7 *(Moderate Change)*

**The issue:** By Day 5, your oldest non-answerers have had 8+ attempts but are only being called twice a day. With 6 days still to go, there's room to increase pressure.

**The fix:** Start the midday sweep on Day 5 (not Day 7 as in the base sequence), applying it to all leads who haven't answered from Days 1–3. This gives your most stubborn non-answerers an extra daily touch during the peak mid-sequence window.

**Trade-off:** More calls per rep per day from Day 5 onward — assess team capacity before implementing.

---

## Option 3 — Triage Non-Responders After Day 6 *(Bigger Change)*

**The issue:** A lead who hasn't picked up after 10+ calls across 6 days is unlikely to answer a cold call. Continuing to double dial them at the same rate as newer leads may be burning your team's time.

**The fix:** After Day 6, split your non-answerers into two tracks:
- **Track A (Active):** Leads from Days 4–6 onward — continue double-dialling as normal, morning + evening.
- **Track B (Text-only):** Leads from Days 1–3 who have had 8+ unanswered attempts — switch to text/WhatsApp only (no more calls). Free up call time to focus on higher-probability leads.

Days 7–10, your callers focus entirely on Track A leads and any new leads, while Track B gets a text every 1–2 days.

**Trade-off:** Some Track B leads may have been meaning to pick up — you'll miss them. But you'll get more quality time on leads more likely to convert.
