Tool Evaluation

Screencap — can it record a workday & help build automations?

Quick summary for the team on whether Screencap fits our two use cases.

🔗 screencap.sh

What it is

A local-first, source-available macOS app that runs in the background and automatically captures screen activity, clicks, keystrokes, and window context. An on-device AI model segments the whole day into labeled tasks — the “Moments” you see in the app (e.g. “Payroll run,” “Browser Issue”). Everything is auto-named, tagged, searchable, and exportable.

Auto-segmented Tagged Searchable Exportable Local-first

Our two use cases

✓

Record an employee’s screen for the day

Yes — directly. This is the core function. Runs in the background on a schedule, no manual start/stop, and produces a reviewable timeline of the full day.

~

Record screen and create automations for the work

Partly. It captures and structures the raw workflow (“turn real workflows into AI training data,” with MCP support to feed AI agents). It does not itself build or run bots that do the work — it gives you the source material you’d feed into a separate automation/AI tool.

Key details

  • PlatformmacOS only today (Windows / browser / Android listed as coming soon). Everyone needs a Mac.
  • PricingFree for personal use · 7-day business trial · then $9/mo per user for team use.
  • PrivacyLocal-first — recordings stay on the machine unless shared. Sensitive apps can be blocked or masked before anything is written to disk. E2E encryption for sharing; “App rules” to exclude personal browsers, banking, etc.
  • SourceSource-available on GitHub.

⚠️ Before we deploy to employees

Recording staff screens/keystrokes usually carries legal notice-and-consent requirements that vary by country/state. We should disclose the monitoring and get whatever consent our jurisdiction requires before rollout. The app’s privacy controls help, but they don’t replace that obligation.
