# Masterclass Follow-Up SOP

## Day of Masterclass (after it completes)

- Call all **Category 1 attendees** who showed up and did not book a call. Make sure to double dial. Make sure to follow **SOP 4** and leave a text if they don't show up.
- If there is time, feel free to double dial the same attendees who didn't book a call — if it has been at least three hours since the first time you dialled.
- If you have time, start calling by double dialling all the **Category 2 people** who did not show up and did not book a call. Make sure to follow **SOP 4** and leave a text if they don't pick up.

## 1 Day After Masterclass

1. Double dial all **Category 1 attendees** who showed up and did not book a call.
2. Double dial all the **Category 2 people** who registered and did not show up and did not book a call.
3. If there is time and it has been at least three hours since you first called, then double dial again all the attendees who still have not picked up and not responded, who didn't show up and didn't book a call.
4. Do the same as you did in step two for this category of people.

> **Note:** If you have sent the first non-pickup text and you still have no response, then after the third time you've double dialled, send the corresponding text below.

## 2–4 Days After Masterclass

Repeat the same as **1 Day After the Masterclass** until each person has been double dialled at least **four times** and received at least **two texts** each.
