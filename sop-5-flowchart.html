Objection Handling
Common objection: "Call me back later, I'm busy."
OBJECTION: "Call back later — I'm busy"
▼
You have three response options. Choose based on the vibe of the conversation.
▼
Response Option 1
Direct re-qualify "I hear you and I know that life can get busy. Can I ask — because I know that whatever is a priority in life, people are going to decide to focus on that priority — were you genuinely interested in making $30k per month in the next 90 days as a coach, or not?"
▼
Are they interested?
▼
No
"No problem at all. Keep an eye out for our emails — we have other free invitations and appreciate you letting me know. Have a good day."
▼
End call
Yes
Continue to discovery — go to Option 3 steps or resume main script from Stage 2.
Response Option 2
Rapport builder "Ah cool — that's awesome! What are you up to?"

Ask one or two more general questions about what they're doing, then naturally transition back to the main call script from Stage 1 / Option 1.
▼
After 1–2 rapport questions, pivot back to Stage 2 — Goals.
Response Option 3
News hook "Did you see what happened on the news yesterday? It said it's never been easier to make $30k per month within 90 days as a coach. All it requires is 60 seconds of someone's life for them to receive $30,000."
▼
Step 1 — Programme check "Do you have a programme offer to sell already? It needs to be $2,000–$5,000."
▼
Step 2 — Niche check "Do you have a niche already that you're working with, or are you still trying to figure that out?"

If yes: "Okay — how do you describe your niche and who you're trying to target?"
▼
Step 3 — Find their why "Why do you want this coaching business to work? Why do you want to make $30k per month in the next 90 days doing coaching?"

"What's your work right now? I'm imagining you've thought about this and you don't want to keep doing that work full-time for another 10, 20, or 30 years — is that how it is?"
▼
Step 4 — Set the interview "Okay great — so you've got this goal. You [do/don't] have a programme, and you [have/are figuring out] your niche. What needs to happen now is I need to give you a call with the team so they can help you get clarity on this and get progress happening toward your goal.

Full disclaimer: if they think they can help, they're going to offer you something. If it gets you to $30k per month in 90 days, are you okay with that? It definitely costs less than that amount of money."
▼
Step 5 — Financial qualification "So I know how to give them the right feedback and so they have the best information on how to serve you best — I need to know…"

Ask the financial qualification questions, then continue from the financial qualification branch in the main script.
▼
After qualification, follow the main script: book interview → Stage 5 → EOC Process.
