## SOP: HANDLING OVERDUE STUDENTS AT AN EVENT

### 1. Purpose
To ensure no student enters the event room while overdue on payments, unless they have caught up or committed to an agreed, evidenced payment plan — and to capture and escalate anyone who refuses.

### 2. Before doors open (the night before / early morning Day 1)
- [ ] Confirm the **1–2 people in charge** of the Overdue desk.
- [ ] Each has a **working laptop** and a **way to take payment**:
  - **Stripe** (confirm logins work), **and/or**
  - **Square tapper** (confirm it's charged, connected, and tested with a $1 test if possible).
- [ ] Both people have the **bank details** (Section 7) on hand for students whose card is being blocked.
- [ ] Pull **Sasha's overdue list** from the **NTT Prac channel** and Luke's **"how to take payments"** post.
- [ ] Build the **tracking sheet** (Section 3) — every name off Sasha's list gets a row before doors open.
- [ ] Position the desk so overdue students are caught **before** they enter the room.

### 3. The tracking sheet (single source of truth)
Set up one shared sheet, one row per student, with at least these columns:

| Name | Amount owing | Spoken to? (Y/N) | Who spoke to them | Outcome | Notes (what they said / pay date / plan) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Every student from Sasha's list is entered **before** doors open, all starting as **Spoken to? = N**.
- The row is updated **the moment** a student is dealt with — no batching it up later.
- **Outcome** should be one of: *Paid in full / Caught up*, *Part-paid + committed plan*, *Refused / no payment*, *Escalated to Luke*.
- This sheet is how we prove, at any moment, exactly who has and hasn't been spoken to. Nobody is admitted to the room without a row that shows they've been handled.

### 4. When an overdue student arrives — the process
1. Greet them, check them against the tracking sheet, confirm the amount owing.
2. Explain warmly but firmly: to enter the room they need to either:
   - **Option A — Catch up:** pay their account up to their agreed payment plan, or
   - **Option B — Best-effort + committed plan:** pay the best they can right now **and** lock in a new/adjusted scheduled payment plan, with a **strong verbal + written commitment** to honour it.
3. For Option B, get **evidence of intent** — e.g. confirmed next payment date, plan in writing, deposit taken now. We need confidence they're not going to do the course and run.
4. Take the payment (Stripe / Square / bank transfer using Section 8 if their card is blocked).
5. **Update their row on the tracking sheet immediately** — mark Spoken to = Y, who spoke to them, and the outcome.

### 5. If their card is being blocked
- Common with **international / USA students** whose bank blocks the charge.
- Give them the correct **bank details** (Section 8 — USA or Australia) so they can transfer directly.
- Ask them to **show evidence of the transfer** (screenshot / confirmation) before they enter.

### 6. If they can't/won't meet Option A or B and ask to be let in anyway
- **Do NOT approve entry yourself.** This is above the desk's authority.
- Say, word for word: *"This is outside of the realm of the agreement. I have to escalate it and I have to get back to you."*
- Mark their row **Escalated to Luke** and record:
  - The student's **name**,
  - **What they're saying** (their reason / request),
  - The **date they say they'll pay**,
  - The **scheduled payment plan they're proposing**.
- Do this **as soon as possible** — we want this in real time, not after the event.

### 7. Refusing to pay anything / looks like they won't pay in future
- If a student **refuses to pay anything at all**, or is only offering **extremely low repayments**, or **can't say when they'll pay in future**, **do not let them into the room** and **do not approve** anything.
- **Arrange to talk to them at lunch** — book that conversation in, tell them we'll sit down with them at lunch to sort it out.
- Flag their row clearly so they land on the **lunch list** (Section 3 / Section 10).
- Keep this as a running list through the morning so nothing slips.
